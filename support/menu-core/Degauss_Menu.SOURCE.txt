The Menu retains the upstream 25 MHz clock. The private native framebuffer
advances through a four-in-five pixel clock enable, preserving its 20 MHz
D161 timing units for existing Main executables and custom modelines.
The stock and private readers are mutually exclusive on the DDR port.

The corresponding patched source archive is attached to the Degauss release
as Degauss_Menu-source.tar.gz.
